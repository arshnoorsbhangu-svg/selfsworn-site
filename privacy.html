SELFSWORN

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

SelfSworn is a personal rulebook app made by Arshnoor Singh Bhangu ("we"). What people write in SelfSworn is deeply personal, so the app is built to collect as little as possible.

In short: there are no accounts, your rulebook stays on your phone, there are no ads or analytics, and the only time your writing leaves the phone is when you choose to use the Guide.

No accounts

SelfSworn has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we cannot identify you.

What stays on your phone

Your laws and their stories, nightly check-ins, urge records, quit trackers, journal entries and settings are stored only on your device. There is no copy on a server, and we cannot see any of it.

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN itself is never stored; a scrambled check value is kept in your phone's secure storage. Face ID and fingerprint checks are done by your phone, and SelfSworn never receives your biometric data.

The nightly reminder is scheduled on your phone. It is not sent from a server.

What leaves your phone

The Guide (optional, and only with your consent). The Guide is an AI chat. Before you can use it, the app shows a notice explaining what is sent and asks you to agree. If you agree and send a message:

  • the messages in that conversation, together with your laws, their stories and their consequences, are sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted by Supabase) and passed on to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which writes the reply;
  • a two-letter country code is sent too, so the Guide only mentions support lines for your country. It comes from your App Store region, your phone's region, or the country you chose in Settings;
  • a random identifier for your install of the app is sent, so we can limit how many messages each device sends per day.

We do not store Guide conversations, and the app does not save them either: a conversation is gone when you leave the screen. To prevent misuse, our server keeps a daily count of Guide messages against a scrambled form of the install identifier and of your network (IP) address. These counts cannot be turned back into either, contain nothing you wrote, and are deleted after 30 days. Anthropic processes what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy. If you are not comfortable with this, do not use the Guide; everything else in SelfSworn works without it.

Subscriptions. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card or other payment details. To confirm that a subscription is active, the app sends our server Apple's transaction reference and the random install identifier; we keep a scrambled form of that identifier, the transaction reference and the date the subscription runs until.

App updates. When it opens, the app checks Expo's update service for a newer version of itself. That request carries technical details such as the app version and platform, and nothing you have written.

Sharing and export. If you share a law or export a copy of your data, it goes wherever you choose to send it.

As with any internet service, the companies that host these servers may briefly log technical details of a request, such as its IP address and time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Erasing your data

In the app, open More, then Settings, then choose "Erase everything". This removes your rulebook, check-ins, trackers, journal, settings, PIN, reminder and the install identifier from the phone, and returns the app to how it was when first installed. Deleting the app does the same. "Export a copy" in the same place lets you keep a record first.

Because nothing you write is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete there. The message counts described above expire on their own after 30 days.

Age

SelfSworn is for people aged 18 and over, and asks you to confirm this when it first opens. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Not a health service

SelfSworn supports personal change. It is not medical treatment, therapy or a crisis service, and the Guide is not a doctor or counsellor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. The app lists crisis and support lines for your country.

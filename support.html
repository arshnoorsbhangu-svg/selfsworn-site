Support

SelfSworn is a personal rulebook and daily habit list: you write the laws you want to live by, each with the story behind it, and tick them off alongside your daily habits.

For help with the app, email arshnoorsbhangu@gmail.com.

Subscriptions

How do I restore my purchase? Open Me, then SelfSworn Premium, and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose SelfSworn and tap Cancel. You can also get there from Me, SelfSworn Premium, "Manage or cancel subscription". Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I erase my data? Open Me, then Settings, then "Erase everything". This wipes your rulebook, PIN and settings from the phone. Deleting the app does the same.

I got a new phone. Where is my rulebook? Your data lives only on the phone it was written on; there are no accounts and no cloud copy. Use Settings, then "Export a copy" on the old phone to keep a record.

I forgot my PIN. A PIN cannot be recovered, because we never have it. On the lock screen choose "Forgot your PIN?" to erase SelfSworn's data and start again.

The Guide

Is the Guide medical help? No. The Guide is an AI coach for wording laws and thinking things through. It is not a doctor, therapist or crisis service, it cannot diagnose or treat anything, and it can be wrong. For health questions, speak to a professional.

Why did the Guide stop replying? There is a daily message limit: 5 a day for free, 40 a day with Premium. It opens again the next day.

If you need to talk to someone

SelfSworn is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Crisis and support lines are inside the app: open Me, then "Help and support lines", to see free lines for your country. There are checked lists for Australia, Canada, India, Ireland, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and the United States. In every other country the app links to Find A Helpline, a free international directory.

The app is showing the wrong country. It uses your App Store region, then your phone's region. To change it, open Me, Settings, then Support lines.
